Non-anaesthetic, worry-free teeth cleaning.

Your pet will not be sedated in any way while getting their teeth cleaned with us. Our groomers are trained to handle dogs during their teeth cleaning session, ensuring a stress-free and comfortable process for them! We also do not use any electrical equipment, minimising discomfort caused by vibrations and loud noises.

Is teeth cleaning necessary?

Over time, plaque and tartar can accumulate on your dog's teeth, acting as a breeding ground for bacteria. This cannot be removed by regular brushing. Eventually, you may notice bad breath and stained discoloured teeth on your dog. We recommend teeth cleaning at least once a year for adult dogs.

What if my dog is elderly or struggles?

We will allow your dog to calm down for a while after you drop them off before beginning the teeth cleaning process. We believe in taking it at their pace, giving them ample breaks in between working on their teeth! A teeth cleaning session can last up to 2 hours. However, if your dog shows severe discomfort or stress during the process, we may not be able to complete the teeth cleaning.
