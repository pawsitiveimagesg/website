About us

Established in 2015, our grooming salon brings together over a decade of hands-on experience in caring for pets. We strive to create an environment where you can entrust your pets' overall wellbeing with us.

Who we are

Our Story

Pawsitive Image was envisioned by two dog lovers. We have always wanted to open a one-stop solution for pet grooming needs. We finally got started on 8/8/2015. We strive to create a safe environment to make your pets feel at home. We are also dedicated to create a pawsitive image for your furry pals.

Our experienced groomers will check on your pet's condition and update pet owners about the grooming process. We will always apply a gentle and loving touch to make your pets well groomed and beautiful, one pet at a time!
