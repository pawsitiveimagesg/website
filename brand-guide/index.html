1 · The short version

The brand in one minute

Pawsitive Image is a premium, specialist dog grooming salon. Not the cheapest and not the fastest.

What we lead withCraft backed by experienceHand-scissored grooming by award-winning groomers, since 2015.
Our one lineHand-scissored grooming, since 2015Sits under the name and opens the homepage.
Who we talk toPremium dog owners near UbiAny breed. People who care how the result looks and how their dog is treated.
How we soundCalm expert, warmFacts over adjectives. No puns, no baby talk.
How we lookNavy, cream and a little goldSerif headings and real photos of our own work.
What we never doDiscounts, puns, medical claims, stock photos

What premium means here: fewer dogs a day, more time per dog, and the same result every visit. Every choice below is meant to make that believable.

The message, always in this order

3 · Logo

Two marks, each with a job

Your cartoon logo stays exactly as it is. We add a simple typeset name for the places a new customer sees first.

Pawsitive ImageHand-scissored grooming, since 2015
Main mark (new)The name, typeset in navy Used on the website header, price list, ads, booking confirmations and documents.
Badge (your current logo)The cat and dog mascot, unchanged Used on the shopfront, stickers, loyalty cards, the social profile picture and the website footer. No signage needs to change.

Rules

  • Don't redraw, recolour or crop the mascot.
  • Don't put the mascot in the website header or at the top of an ad.
  • Don't show both marks side by side.
  • Don't use the mascot's red as a brand colour anywhere else.

6 · Photos

Our own work only. No stock photos.

Photos are the proof behind "hand-scissored". Five kinds of shot:

The finishThe groomed dog alone on a plain cream or navy backdrop, at eye level, in soft light.
The handsClose-ups of scissoring and finishing. This shows the craft.
Before and afterSame dog, same angle, same backdrop, same distance.
The groomerThe head groomer and team at work, calm and focused, in uniform.
The salonClean, tidy, well lit, with a groomer beside each dog.

Rules

  • Ask the owner before posting their dog. Use the dog's first name in the caption.
  • Keep backgrounds clear of clutter, cages, towels and price signs.
  • No stock, AI-made or borrowed photos.
  • No costumes, props, filters, stickers or text over the dog.
  • No dog that looks stressed or restrained, and no dog pictured alone on a table.
  • No "before" photos that embarrass the owner.
7 · How we sound

Calm expert, warm

We sound like a skilled groomer explaining their work to a client they respect.

Plain and specificVague or flowery
ConfidentBoastful
KindCutesy
Honest about limitsSalesy
  • Short sentences. One idea each.
  • Say what happens, how long it takes and what it costs.
  • Say "we" and "your dog". On WhatsApp, use the dog's name.
  • No emoji on the website. At most one in a WhatsApp message or caption.
  • The pun lives in the name only. "Pawsitive" is the brand name; there is no other wordplay anywhere.

9 · Prices and offers

No discounts. We add value instead.

This is the biggest change from today, so please comment if you disagree.

  • Prices are shown openly as "from $X" by size and coat.
  • No percent-off, festive promotions, flash deals or "promo" banners.
  • An offer is something added, not money taken off. For example, a complimentary spa add-on on a first visit, or a perk for rebooking at checkout.
  • We explain what changes a price (matting, size, coat, temperament) before the groom, not after.
  • The "current promotions" part of the prices page becomes "What's included" and "Rebooking perks".
If a client asks for a discountWe keep one price for everyone so every dog gets the same time and care.
10 · What we can claim

Care claims only, never medical

This protects the business. A grooming salon is not a vet, and the wording must show that.

  • We describe what is done and what the owner will see.
  • We never say treats, cures, prevents, heals or vet-grade.
  • Every teeth-cleaning and spa page says when to see a vet first: loose teeth, bleeding gums, painful bad breath, open sores or skin infection.
  • Awards and numbers are exact: the award's name, year and who gave it; the real Google rating and review count.
  • We compare methods (clipper cut against hand-scissoring), never other salons by name.
  • Teeth cleaning is not promoted until a vet or AVS confirms what a non-vet may do and say in Singapore.
11 · Groomers

The team's standard, with the head groomer named

  • The brand owns the standard: "the Pawsitive Image finish". Every dog leaves to that standard, whoever worked on it.
  • The head groomer is named and pictured, with the award and years of experience, as the person who sets and checks the finish.
  • Other groomers appear by first name and certification on the About page.
  • We don't promise a specific groomer in marketing, and we don't write "book with" a named person.
12 · Cats

Dogs lead for now

Cat grooming is still offered and keeps its own page. For now, cats do not appear in headlines, the one line or the main photos.

We will add a cat section only after testing cat demand with your existing clients.
