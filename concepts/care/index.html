Questions

Things owners ask us

My dog is nervous. Can you still groom them?

Yes. We give your dog time to settle after drop-off and take breaks during the groom. If your dog shows severe stress, we stop and tell you.

Will my dog be left in a cage?

A groomer stays with your dog throughout the groom. Tell us if your dog has any worries and we will plan around them.

How often should my dog be groomed?

Most dogs do well on a four to six week cycle. We will suggest a date at pick-up and can hold the same slot for you.

When should I see a vet instead?

If your dog has open sores, a skin infection, loose teeth or bleeding gums, please see your vet first.
