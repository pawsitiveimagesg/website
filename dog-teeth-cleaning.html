Non-anaesthetic, worry-free teeth cleaning

Your dog will not be sedated in any way while getting their teeth cleaned with us. Our groomers are trained to handle dogs during teeth cleaning, keeping the process stress-free and comfortable. We also don't use any electrical equipment, which avoids discomfort from vibrations and loud noises.

Is teeth cleaning necessary?

Over time, plaque and tartar build up on your dog's teeth and become a breeding ground for bacteria. Regular brushing can't remove it. Eventually you may notice bad breath and stained, discoloured teeth. We recommend teeth cleaning at least once a year for adult dogs.

What if my dog is elderly or struggles?

After you drop your dog off, we let them calm down before we begin, and we work at their pace with plenty of breaks. A session can last up to 2 hours. If your dog shows severe discomfort or stress, we may not be able to complete the cleaning.

How much does it cost?
